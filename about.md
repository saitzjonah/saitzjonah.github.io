## About My Portfolio

On the whole, engineering portfolios and project write-ups are too polished. 

It is desirable for the final product to be polished, but if any real engineering was done, then the process that led to that final product was decidedly nonlinear. It is not enough to pay lip service to successes, failures, and tradeoffs. If the goal is to teach, we must document these successes, failures, and tradeoffs relentlessly. 

To the uninitiated, no detail is too trivial, and every glossed over assumption is an obstacle to reproducibility. 

It is in this spirit that we proceed.
